Cookie Policy

Last Updated: January 17, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Nezzo uses cookies and similar technologies.

1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that allow us to remember your login state and preferences.

2. How We Use Cookies

2.1. Strictly Necessary Cookies (Essential)

These are required for the platform to function. You cannot switch them off.

  • Authentication: To keep you logged in as you move between the "Agent Builder" and "Workflow Dashboard."
  • Security: To prevent Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF).

2.2. Functional Cookies

  • UI State: To remember if you left the sidebar open or closed, or which "Workflow" you were last editing (stored in LocalStorage).

2.3. Analytics Cookies (Optional)

We use third-party tools (e.g., PostHog, Google Analytics) to understand which features of the Agent Builder are most popular. You can opt-out of these.

3. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies via your browser settings or our "Cookie Preferences" menu in the footer.
